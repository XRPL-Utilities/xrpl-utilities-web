How this works ▼

What's on the feed and why

News, but only the real ones

Three buckets, no social-media noise:

  • Regulators · SEC, FCA, Financial Stability Board
  • Central banks · Fed, ECB, BoE, BoJ, BoC, RBA, BIS
  • Crypto press · XRP / RLUSD / XRPL / Ripple coverage only

No social media or paid placements. A story carried by two sources within 48 hours is marked cross-verified.

On-chain, in real time

XRP and RLUSD payments of $1M or more, with both addresses, the dollar value at ledger close, and a label when either side is a known exchange, market maker, treasury or bridge.

Real-world-asset mints and redemptions arrive as a second stream. Permissioned-domain activity is on XR-Trust.

Who reads it

Traders checking whether news moved money on the ledger, researchers matching announcements to settlement, and agents that want both in one call. We don't rate sources; we only record who carried a story.

How it works

Two streams, one feed: curated news + on-chain whale activity

News. Every item must mention Ripple, XRP, RLUSD or XRPL. When two sources carry the same story within 48 hours, they merge into one cross-verified record that lists both links. We note the XRP price when a story arrives and again four hours later. That is a measurement, not a claim that the news moved the price.

On-chain. Each large payment is one event with its transaction hash, currency, both addresses and any known label (Binance, Coinbase, bitFlyer and so on). One transaction in, one event out.

Real-world assets. A tracked issuer gets an event when it mints or redeems, or when its supply moves between readings. Supply-change events catch mints made through the XRPL exchange rather than a direct payment, and are labeled as such.

# pip install x402-xrpl
from x402_xrpl import X402RequestsSession
from xrpl.wallet import Wallet

def pay_in_xrp(accepts, *_):
    return next(a for a in accepts
                if a["network"] == "xrpl:0" and a["asset"] == "XRP")

session = X402RequestsSession(
    wallet=Wallet.from_seed(seed),
    rpc_url="https://YOUR_XRPL_RPC",
    payment_requirements_selector=pay_in_xrp,
)

resp = session.post(
    "https://pulse.xrpl-utilities.io/events/recent",
    json={"limit": 50},
)
resp.raise_for_status()  # a 402 here means the payment did not go through
feed = resp.json()

for e in feed["events"]:
    print(e["title"], e["source_appearances"])

Output shape

What you get back

  • events[]: news and on-chain activity in one list, newest first
  • Each event has a title, link, short summary and time
  • source_appearances[] and appearance_count: which outlets or on-chain feeds reported it, and how many
  • News events carry the XRP price when the story landed and 4 hours later. A measurement, not a claim that one caused the other.
  • target_addresses[]: the wallets involved, with XR-Sentinel context where it has some
  • min_whale_usd: the smallest payment included (default $1M; pass 0 to see every stored payment of $50,000 or more).

XR-Pulse is a measurement-only event feed, not an editorial product. Source presence is reported as a property of an event, not a verdict on the source. Correlation against XR-Telemetry is a measurement, not a causal claim.
