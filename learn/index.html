Learn the XRP Ledger

Eight short lessons on how the XRP Ledger works, written for anyone who has heard of Bitcoin and wants to know what else is out there. Every fact links to the official XRP Ledger docs, and every lesson shows you something real on the ledger that you can check yourself.

  1. Lesson 1What is the XRP Ledger?The XRP Ledger is a shared record of payments and balances that computers all over the world keep identical copies of. A new page gets added about every four seconds, and no single company owns it.
