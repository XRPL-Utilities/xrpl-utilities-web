How this works ▼

What is tracked. A real-world asset token is an on-chain claim on something held off-chain: a dollar, a Treasury bill, a fund share, a megawatt-hour. Issuers here include Ripple (RLUSD), Ondo (OUSG), Franklin Templeton (sgBENJI), Aviva Investors, Circle, SG-FORGE (EURCV), Braza and Justoken. Each issuer wallet is checked on the ledger before it is added.

How supply is counted. Supply is what the issuer has put into circulation, in the token's own unit. Where an issuer keeps tokens in its own treasury wallet (OpenEden TBL, for example), those are left out. A token held almost entirely by one or two wallets is marked 1 HOLDER or CONCENTRATED. Most tokens are read from the ledger several times an hour. MPT tokens, the charts and the 7 and 30 day figures use one reading a day, taken just after midnight UTC.

Dollar figures. Only the headline and the dollar charts use prices. Everything else stays in native units.
