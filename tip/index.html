XRPL transactions are public. If you tip, the sending wallet address, amount, and timestamp will be visible on xrpscan.com, bithomp.com, and any other XRPL explorer. There is no anonymous-tip path here. If you want privacy, do not send a tip from a wallet you have associated with your name.
