Active Float

3-second settlement supply

Circulating supply is all XRP outside Ripple's escrow. Modeled Active Float estimates how much of it could trade within one ledger close of 3 to 5 seconds. Most XRP at exchanges sits in customer balances rather than on the order book, so the active slice is far smaller.

Burst Math

Required equilibrium price calculator

If a float of M XRP turns over V times a year at a price of P dollars, it settles Q = P × V × M dollars a year. Set Q and V, take M from the live float, and the calculator solves for P: the XRP price that settlement alone would require. It is a what-if, not a forecast.

# pip install requests
import time
import requests

quote = requests.post(
    "https://telemetry.xrpl-utilities.io/quote",
    json={},
).json()

# Sign & submit XRPL Payment from your wallet using quote["deep_link"]
# Then poll until paid:
while not requests.get(
    f"https://telemetry.xrpl-utilities.io/status/{quote['invoice_id']}"
).json()["paid"]:
    time.sleep(5)

payload = requests.get(
    f"https://telemetry.xrpl-utilities.io/results/{quote['invoice_id']}"
).json()

print(payload["supply"])
print(payload["utility_floor"])

Output shape

What you get back

  • supply: total, circulating, escrowed, dormant and AMM-locked XRP, XRP on exchanges, and 24-hour exchange outflow
  • liquidity: XRP in, out and net by region over 24 hours, with the top venues
  • amm: the largest AMM pools with their size, depth and yield
  • derived_models: XRP available to trade, split into what sits on the ledger and an estimate for exchanges
  • utility_floor: the price-floor calculator’s result from stated assumptions, next to the current price
  • generated_at: when the snapshot was built

Safe harbor & compliance

XR-Telemetry produces on-chain telemetry and an algebraic implied-floor computation. Not a price prediction, investment recommendation, or financial advice. Burst Math and Floor Scenarios are sensitivity tools. They show what the math implies under explicit assumptions, not what XRP will or should be priced at.
