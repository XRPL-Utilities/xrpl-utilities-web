Signal glossary

What do these signal flags mean?

Click to expand. 58 flags in plain English.

▾

Each scan returns a list of these flags. None of them is a risk, compliance or sanctions finding. The exact rules are in the signal catalog.

Behavioral pattern

OFFER_HEAVY_BOT
More than half of its activity is placing and cancelling DEX orders. Typical of a market-making bot.
MULTI_CURRENCY_GATEWAY
Touched four or more different currencies. Typical of a gateway, issuer or multi-asset service.
BURST_ACTIVITY
20 or more transactions packed into less than an hour.
TRUSTLINE_ONLY
More than half of its transactions set up trustlines rather than move money. Typical of issuer or token setup.
NET_OUTBOUND_SWEEP
A High-activity wallet with more than 1.5 times as many outgoing transactions as incoming. Looks like a hot wallet paying out.
NET_INBOUND_ACCUMULATION
A Medium- or High-activity wallet with more than 1.5 times as many incoming transactions as outgoing. An accumulation pattern.
PASSIVE_COUNTERPARTY
Acts like an issuer, gateway or AMM pool that others transact with. Some signals that don't fit those roles are switched off.

Cadence & trajectory

DORMANT_REAWAKENING
Silent for 30 days or more before its recent activity.
SCORE_TRAJECTORY_BOT_ONBOARDING
The activity score rose 30 or more points within 30 days of the last scan and is now High. Needs a previous scan.
CADENCE_TIGHTENING
The typical gap between transactions has at least halved since the last scan. Needs a previous scan.

Counterparties

LABELED_EXCHANGE_COUNTERPARTY
Trades with a wallet publicly labeled as an exchange. Says nothing about compliance.
COUNTERPARTY_INSTITUTIONAL
Trades with a publicly labeled institution: an exchange, issuer, treasury, market maker, bridge or custodian.
INSTITUTIONAL_SCALE_FLOW
$10M or more in XRP and RLUSD payments with a single counterparty in the window, whoever that counterparty is.
ESCROW_COUNTERPARTY_INSTITUTIONAL
$10M or more in XRP and RLUSD escrows with a single counterparty in the window.
BRIDGE_INTERACTION
Has sent to or received from a labeled cross-chain bridge wallet, so value moves to or from other chains.
SINGLE_COUNTERPARTY_HEAVY
At least 80% of its payments (10 or more) are with one address. Typical of a dedicated settlement wallet.
COUNTERPARTY_BURST
10 or more new names in its top counterparties since the last scan. Can mean a payout fan-out or new trading relationships. Needs a previous scan.
COUNTERPARTY_ADVISORY
A top counterparty carries a public advisory, such as a community report of a hack or scam. Worth a closer look.
COUNTERPARTY_ADVISORY_TRUSTED
A counterparty's advisory is marked verified by the source. No public advisory carries that mark today.

Issuer governance & configuration

PERMISSIONED_ISSUER_GOVERNANCE
An issuer that must approve every holder and can claw back balances. Common for tokenized treasuries and regulated stablecoins.
VERIFIED_INSTITUTIONAL_ENTITY
The wallet belongs to an identified institution, either through a verified public label or our registry of known asset issuers. Identity, not conduct.
FIXED_SUPPLY_ISSUER
An issuer that has locked its own keys (blackholed), so no more of its token can ever be minted.
INSTITUTIONAL_BY_CONFIGURATION
Set up like an institution: a multi-sig with 8 or more signers or with key-custody details on its signers, or an allowlist of who may pay it.
DEEP_FROZEN_BY_ISSUER
An issuer has deep-frozen one of its trustlines, so it can neither send nor receive that token. Issuers do this for many reasons.
PERMISSIONED_DEX_PARTICIPANT
Has at least one open order inside a permissioned trading venue (XLS-81), open only to credentialed traders.
OPERATES_PERMISSIONED_DOMAIN
Runs one or more permissioned domains (XLS-80) and has issued credentials under them. Details come from XR-Trust.
WHALE_RECENT_ACTIVITY
Sent or received at least one whale-sized payment in the last 30 days, as recorded by XR-Pulse.
ESCROW_LIFE_HEAVY
At least 30% of its transactions (3 or more in total) are escrow operations. Typical of vesting or settlement wallets.

Provenance & lineage

FRESH_WALLET
Activated in the last 30 days.
WHALE_GENESIS
Funded with 50,000 XRP or more when it was activated. Institutional setup rather than a retail start.
INSTITUTIONAL_PROVENANCE
Its chain of activating wallets leads back to a publicly labeled exchange or institution within 3 steps.
LAYERED_PROVENANCE
Activated through two or more unlabeled wallets in a row, each under 90 days old at the time. Legitimate setups can look like this too.

Address-specific flags

TARGET_ADVISORY
The scanned address itself carries a public advisory. Check it before you transact.
TARGET_ADVISORY_TRUSTED
The advisory on the scanned address is marked verified by the source. No public advisory carries that mark today.
DUST_ONLY_ON_QUIET_WALLET
Every payment is under one cent on an otherwise quiet wallet. Often address poisoning.
DUST_ON_FRESH_WALLET
A wallet with 5 or fewer payments has received at least one under one cent. A common poisoning move on new wallets.
HIGH_DUST_RATIO
More than 20% of its payments (10 or more) are under one cent.
ISSUES_MPT
Issues a Multi-Purpose Token (XLS-33), the newer token type used for stablecoins and tokenized assets.
HOLDS_MPT
Holds a Multi-Purpose Token.
OPERATES_PRICE_ORACLE
Publishes an on-chain price feed (XLS-47).
BRIDGE_OPERATOR
Runs one side of a cross-chain bridge (XLS-38), not just uses one.
HAS_DID
Has published a decentralized identifier on the ledger (XLS-40).
CREDENTIAL_HOLDER
Holds an issued credential (XLS-70), which lets it take part in permissioned venues.
HOLDS_CHECKS
Is party to an XRPL Check, a deferred payment closer to an invoice than a transfer.
PAYMENT_CHANNEL_OPERATOR
Runs an XRPL payment channel, the rail for streaming micropayments.
TOKEN_ESCROW_USER
Has a token, not XRP, locked in escrow (XLS-85). Used for settlement and vesting.
GLOBAL_FREEZE_ACTIVE
The issuer has frozen every trustline to its token at once.
NO_FREEZE_COMMITTED
The issuer has permanently given up the power to freeze its token.
PUNITIVE_TRANSFER_RATE
The issuer charges more than 0.5% when holders transfer its token to each other.
DIRECT_CLAWBACK_ACTOR
Has actually clawed back a holder's balance in the window, not just kept the option.
HIGH_FAILURE_RATE
More than 30% of its transactions (10 or more) failed. Typical of testing, a broken bot, or orders that keep getting rejected.
AMM_LIQUIDITY_PROVIDER
AMM deposits, withdrawals, votes and bids are at least 30% of its activity (3 or more). Supplies liquidity to pools.
NFT_MARKETPLACE_ACTIVE
At least 10 NFT operations, making up 20% or more of its activity.
MULTI_ASSET_ISSUER
Issues three or more different currencies that are in circulation.
WHALE_ACCUMULATOR
At least 80% of its whale-sized payments in 30 days (5 or more) were inbound.
WHALE_DISTRIBUTOR
At least 80% of its whale-sized payments in 30 days (5 or more) were outbound.
WHALE_INSTITUTIONAL_TIER
Two or more large payments in 30 days that either involved a known institution or were $10M or more.
IDENTITY_ATTESTED_INSTITUTION
Its published identity names an organization and two or more people. Self-published, not independently checked.
How this works ▼

Sentinel reads a wallet's last 90 days on the ledger and returns two separate 0 to 100 scores. The activity score shows how automated the wallet looks, with a level of Low, Medium, High, Dormant or Unknown. The institutional posture score adds up the institutional signals it found, and lists each one with its weight. You also get the signal flags and a short AI-written summary.

The two scores are independent. A busy retail bot can score high on activity and zero on posture; a quiet issuer can do the reverse.

Neither score is a risk score, sanctions screen, AML finding or credit rating. A High activity level usually means an exchange, market maker or bot, not a warning.

For developers and AI agents

The same data is available as an API and as MCP tools. Some calls are free; paid calls cost $0.10.

Pricing and API Sentinel Swagger
API details ▼
  • POST /scan · $0.10 · the full report for one address
  • POST /scan/history · $0.10 · up to 25 earlier scans of an address, to see how it changed
  • GET /verify-origin · free · does a domain control the XRPL account it advertises
  • Bulk scan · up to 50 addresses in one payment

Paid calls use x402: the first request answers 402 with the price, your agent pays, then repeats the request. Pay in RLUSD or XRP on the XRP Ledger, or USDC on Base. No API key.

The same calls are MCP tools: xrpl_sentinel_scan, xrpl_sentinel_scan_history and xrpl_sentinel_verify_origin (free), in @xrpl-utilities/mcp or at mcp.xrpl-utilities.io/mcp.

A report carries activity_score, activity_level, institutional_posture, confidence, signals, features, genesis (who activated the wallet), top_counterparties (the top 20) and the AI reasoning. When an earlier scan is on record, a _delta block shows what changed.

Safe harbor & compliance

XR-Sentinel produces behavioral pattern classification, not risk assessment. It is not a compliance, AML, sanctions, or forensic service. A "High" activity level is not a risk signal. It typically indicates an exchange, market maker, AMM, validator, or trading bot.

Users are responsible for complying with local digital asset and AI regulations, including the California Digital Financial Assets Law and the Colorado AI Act where applicable.
