1. Scope of Service

XRPL-Utilities runs data services for the XRP Ledger. They are free to use on xrpl-utilities.com and paid per call through the APIs on xrpl-utilities.io. The services include, but are not limited to, XR-Sentinel™, XR-Pulse™, XR-Telemetry™, XR-Trust™, XR-Vault™, XR-Flows™, and XR-Lineage™.

2. Nature of Data

Scores, flags and figures are produced by deterministic algorithms and heuristic pattern matching over on-chain data. XR-Sentinel™ narrative text (the reasoning field) is generated by a third-party large language model from those signals, may contain errors, and is not reviewed by a human before delivery.

No accuracy guarantee. While We aim for accuracy, blockchain data and behavioral classifications are subject to interpretation. We do not guarantee the 100% accuracy of wallet classifications or "Whale" event reporting.

Third-party content. XR-Pulse™ aggregates institutional news from third-party sources alongside on-chain XRPL activity. We are not responsible for the accuracy, legitimacy, or reliability of external broadcasts.

On-chain identity claims. XR-Trust™ indexes XLS-70/80/81 ledger objects and surfaces self-published off-chain identity from XLS-40 DID records and .well-known/xrp-ledger.toml manifests when an operator publishes one. Inclusion in the directory is not an endorsement, certification, or compliance verdict on any domain, owner, credential issuer, or member; absence is not a negative judgment. We do not verify the truthfulness of self-attested identity claims. Always do your own research and independently verify any operator before transacting or extending credit.

Real-world asset figures. XR-Vault™ reports on-chain mint, burn, and circulating supply for tokenized real-world assets in each token's own unit. Dollar totals on the dashboard are estimates, not on-chain prices: dollar tokens at $1, fund and Treasury shares at their latest published price, and tokens in other currencies at recent exchange rates. Tokens with no reliable price stay in their own units and are left out of dollar totals. Other issuer-stated metrics (marketed AUM, off-chain redemption status, NAV, yield) are not surfaced unless operator-attested and labeled as such. Inclusion of an issuer is not an endorsement of the underlying real-world asset or its custodian. Always do your own research and independently verify any RWA issuer before allocating capital.

Unverified RWA candidates. The XR-Vault™ dashboard surfaces a "Recently discovered" section listing wallets whose public on-chain settings and issued supply look like a regulated RWA issuer but that we have not yet reviewed. Candidates are detected automatically from public ledger data and public wallet labels. Candidate rows show chain-derived fields only (wallet, label, domain, currency, obligations, trustline count, on-chain flags); the asset class, the marketing-friendly name, and any per-issuer caveats are deliberately not auto-populated. Inclusion as a candidate is not an endorsement, certification, verification, or compliance verdict on any wallet, issuer, asset, or counterparty. A candidate may be removed from the dashboard at any time, including if operator review determines the wallet is not a real-world asset issuer. The "Unverified" badge and the candidate-specific tile styling exist to make this distinction visible at a glance; consumers should treat the canonical tracked-issuer set and the candidate set as categorically different.

ETF flow figures. XR-Flows™ tracks XRP exchange-traded funds and products listed in the US and Europe, both spot funds and baskets that hold XRP alongside other assets. Net asset values come from each issuer's public figures, updated daily. We do not cross-validate issuer-reported AUM against on-chain spot price, custodian attestations, or third-party fund administrators, and per-source freshness is exposed on each /scan response so consumers can audit lineage. ETFs hold XRP through pooled institutional custodians, so on-chain wallet activity cannot be cleanly attributed to a specific fund; correlation between ETF AUM movement and XRPL exchange-flow delta is a directional measurement, not a causal claim. Inclusion of an ETF in the tracked registry is not investment advice, an endorsement, or a recommendation. The auto-discovery surface at /stats/etf-candidates is the same kind of pre-review queue as XR-Vault™'s unverified candidates: visibility into pipeline, not endorsement.

Access to specific data payloads is gated by the x402 (HTTP 402 "Payment Required") protocol.

Fees. Most paid calls cost $0.10. Bulk XR-Sentinel™ scans cost $0.10 per address. XR-Pulse™ live streams cost $0.50 for 1 hour, $2.50 for 6 hours and $7.50 for 24 hours. Every paid route states its exact price in its 402 response, and the full list is on the Pricing page. Using the websites is free.

Finality. Payments made via native XRP or RLUSD on XRPL, or USDC on Base mainnet, are settled on-chain and are final once the payload is delivered.

Refunds. You pay only for data that is delivered. If a paid request fails before settlement, the signed payment is never submitted and you are not charged. Settled payments are final and are not refunded.

Non-custodial. XRPL-Utilities LLC never requests, stores, or interacts with your private keys. All payments must be initiated by the user or their authorized agent.
