Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 5, 2026

XRPL-Utilities LLC runs xrpl-utilities.com, the APIs on xrpl-utilities.io and the XRPL-Utilities MCP server. This page explains what we collect when you use them, why, and what we do with it.

1. What we collect

  • Network data. When you load a page or call an API, our servers and hosting providers see your IP address and browser or client details (user agent). We use the IP address to limit abuse. Our services turn it into a one-way hashed value for rate limiting and do not store the raw address in our databases. Our hosting providers may keep standard access logs for a short time.
  • Bot check. The website uses Cloudflare Turnstile to check that a visitor is not an automated script before giving out a free visitor pass. Cloudflare processes your IP address and browser signals for this. The pass is kept in your browser's local storage for up to 15 minutes and only works from the network you got it on.
  • Site analytics. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views. It does not use cookies.
  • Payments. If you or your agent pay for an API call, the payment is a public transaction on the XRP Ledger or Base. We record the transaction details (paying wallet address, amount and transaction hash) to confirm the payment and to stop the same payment being used twice. Wallet addresses and transactions on public blockchains are public by design.
  • What you look up. When you look up a wallet, issuer or address, we process the address you entered. Some services keep earlier lookups so they can be served again, for example XR-Sentinel scan history. These are public ledger addresses.
  • Contact form. If you use the contact form, we receive the name, email address and message you enter, through our form provider Web3Forms. We use it only to reply to you.
  • Error reports. When something breaks, our services send error details to Sentry, our error-tracking provider, with personal data turned off. Request bodies, IP addresses and cookies are left out.

We do not create user accounts, we do not sell personal information, and we do not use advertising trackers.

2. AI summaries

XR-Sentinel writes a plain-English summary of a wallet's activity using a third-party AI model. We send that model the public on-chain signals for the wallet being scanned. We do not send it your IP address or contact details.

3. Who we share data with

We share data only with the providers that run the service for us: our hosting providers (Railway and Cloudflare), Cloudflare Turnstile and Web Analytics, Sentry, Web3Forms, our AI model provider, the payment facilitators that check and settle payments (t54 for the XRP Ledger and Coinbase for Base), and public blockchain nodes. We may also share information when the law requires it.

4. How long we keep it

  • Hashed rate-limit values: in memory only, cleared when a service restarts.
  • Payment records: as long as we need them to block reused payments and for our business records.
  • Contact messages: as long as we need them to answer you, and no longer than two years.
  • Error reports: up to 90 days, under our error-tracking provider's standard retention.

5. Your choices

You can use the website without an account and without sending us anything. You can ask what personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, through the contact page. We cannot change or delete anything written to a public blockchain.

6. Children

Our services are not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

7. Changes

If we change this policy, we update the date at the top of this page.

8. Contact

XRPL-Utilities LLC, Wyoming, USA. Reach us through the contact page.
