Follow any XRP Ledger wallet

Paste a wallet address or a transaction ID. You'll see what it did in plain English: who funded it, where its money went, what tokens it made and who it dealt with. Click any wallet along the way to keep following.

Do your own research

Everything here comes straight from the public XRP Ledger and is shown as it is. We don't judge anyone. A wallet's history can't tell you who owns it or why they did something. Do your own research before you send money to anyone, and never share your secret key. We will never ask for it.

How to read it

1. Start anywhere

A wallet address starts with r. A transaction ID is 64 letters and numbers. Both are public on the XRP Ledger.

2. Read the story

Each line is one thing the wallet did, newest first. Tap Details on any line to see every balance that moved.

3. Follow the money

Tap any blue wallet to jump to it. Your path stays at the top so you can step back.

Names appear only for public exchanges and well-known organizations. They come from public wallet labels and our own research, so they can be wrong. We never name people. Spot a mistake? Tell us. Nothing you search for is saved.
